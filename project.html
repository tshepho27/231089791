Res Hub

Project Introduction

Res Hub is a proposed web-based platform designed to help students find reliable information about off-campus student residences. The system aims to make it easier for students to learn about residences before choosing where to stay.

Problem Statement

Students may struggle to find reliable and honest information about off-campus residences. Important information such as water and electricity availability, maintenance problems, location, safety, transport and the behaviour of landlords or residence managers may not be easy to find.

Project Objectives

  • Provide reliable information about student residences.
  • Allow students to review residences.
  • Help students identify common problems in residences.
  • Provide information about location and transport.
  • Help students make better accommodation decisions.

Main Features

  1. Residence information
  2. Student reviews and ratings
  3. Safety and location information
  4. Maintenance and infrastructure reports
  5. Transport information
  6. Landlord or management responses

Technologies

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • React
  • Node.js
  • Supabase
